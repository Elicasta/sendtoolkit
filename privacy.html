Privacy

Last updated October 4, 2026.

What we collect

If you join product updates, SendToolkit stores the email address you submit so we can send product releases and related operator notes. Checkout providers may collect payment and order details under their own privacy terms.

How we use it

We use signup information to send the emails you asked for, manage subscriptions, and understand basic website and product activity. We do not sell subscriber information.

Email preferences

Marketing emails should include an unsubscribe option. You can use that link to stop future product-update emails.

Service providers

The site may use Vercel for hosting, Resend for email-list management, Stan Store for the current checkout, and Stripe when direct checkout is enabled.

Changes

This page may change as the product and checkout stack change.
